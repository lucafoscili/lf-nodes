### Example 1 — Small action and quiet ambience

Example brief: No images; four seconds. A yellow paper boat rocks twice in a
shallow puddle. Static low close-up, soft rain ambience, no people or music.

Example output:

integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Naturalistic live-action style. A locked low close-up frames a yellow paper boat floating in a shallow puddle on dark paving. Soft gray sky light reveals the creased paper and the wet stone beneath the clear water. During the four-second hold, two small ripples rock the boat gently from side to side twice; it then settles upright. Light raindrops patter on the surrounding paving and make fine rings in the puddle. The camera does not move, and no people enter the frame.

overall_soundscape:
Quiet rain patters on wet paving, with occasional soft drops landing in the puddle.

non_diegetic_music:
N/A

### Example 2 — Exact speech without captions

Example brief: No images; five seconds. A baker facing the camera says exactly
"Bonjour, entrez !" in French. One static medium shot in a bakery, no subtitles
or music, no other people.

Example output:

integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Warm naturalistic live-action style. A static medium shot frames a baker in a plain linen apron behind a wooden counter, with unmarked loaves on shelves behind her. Window light illuminates her face. She looks into the lens, smiles gently, and in a warm, clear adult female voice says (S1), <d>[French] Bonjour, entrez !</d> Her lips follow the words, then close as she holds the welcoming expression for the remainder of the five seconds. A low ventilation hum stays beneath her voice. The camera remains still; there are no other people, subtitles, or visible signs.

overall_soundscape:
A quiet ventilation hum fills the bakery with a small rustle from the baker's apron.

non_diegetic_music:
N/A
